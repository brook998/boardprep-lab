How BoardPrep Lab works

  1. Choose your class, board and subject.Class 10 is CBSE. Class 12 offers CBSE and ISC.
  2. Choose a chapter or mix the available chapters.The question count shows how many questions currently exist in that selection.
  3. Practise or take a timed test.Practice mode gives feedback as you go. Timed test mode checks answers when you submit.
  4. Review the result.Read explanations, revisit mistakes and check your progress dashboard.

This starter site is for learning and practice, not for secure exams. Answer keys are included in the browser files, and progress is stored locally.
